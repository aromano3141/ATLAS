Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026

Who operates the application

Reality Sync runs on the operator’s computer. The operator controls connected accounts, permitted resources, retention, and sharing. This public policy site contains static documents only; it has no application sign-in, evidence upload, analytics script, or application database.

Information processed

The local application can read configured Slack messages, author identifiers and timestamps; Calendar event details, attendees and reminders; and selected Jira release and issue information. It stores original evidence, independent assessments, approval records, job state, and verification receipts locally so interrupted work can recover. It processes reminder requests, selected recipients, and your configured preferences.

Location and mapping information

A trip uses an address or starting point that you provide, or device coordinates after browser permission. It does not infer your current location from calendar history. Origins, destinations, route options, and stop queries are transmitted to Google Maps Platform to calculate routes and find places. Raw mapping responses, geometry, opening details, and availability are transient. The application retains permitted identifiers and your accepted trip and reminder decisions for maintenance. See the Google Privacy Policy.

AI processing and other providers

Relevant source evidence and your requests are sent to OpenAI for claim extraction, independent assessments, and intent interpretation. API requests disable application-requested response storage where supported; OpenAI’s own processing and retention policies still apply. Approved repairs, reminders, and reviewed messages are transmitted to the corresponding Slack, Google, or Atlassian service. Those providers handle data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Storage and sharing

Credentials remain on the local server and are not included in the application repository or browser responses. Local records are kept outside the project’s synced folder. The application does not publish workspace data to this policy site. Shared messages require review and configured audience checks. Access to a source does not by itself authorize redistribution to a different audience.

Retention and your choices

Local operational records remain until the operator removes them. You can pause scans, change preferences, cancel managed reminders where the provider permits, disconnect provider authorizations, or stop the application. To remove local data, first resolve or cancel pending provider schedules, stop the application, then remove its local data folder. Removing local records does not remove copies held by providers, already sent messages, or provider schedules; manage those in the originating service.

Policy site and contact

The hosting provider may process standard request information such as IP address and access logs to deliver these pages. Reality Sync adds no advertising, tracking cookies, or analytics to this policy site. For access, deletion, correction, or privacy questions, contact the operator who provided your installation or demo access through your existing private communication channel.

Updates

Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date. Review this policy before connecting additional accounts or enabling new capabilities.
